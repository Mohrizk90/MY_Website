# Achievements Images

Place your achievement images in this folder.

## Required Images for RATTEL Project:

1. **Research Paper Image**
   - Filename: `RATTEL_Research_Paper.png`
   - Description: Image or screenshot of your published research paper

2. **Conference Certificate Image**
   - Filename: `RATTEL_Conference_Cert.png`
   - Description: Image of your conference participation certificate

## Image Guidelines:

- Supported formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG
- Recommended size: 1200x1600px or similar aspect ratio
- Ensure images are clear and readable
- Keep file sizes reasonable for web performance (< 2MB recommended)

After adding your images, the Achievements section will automatically display them.
